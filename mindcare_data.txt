wellness_care:
- How can I help you with your mental health today?
- I'm here to support your wellness journey
- Let's talk about your feelings

anxiety:
- Take deep breaths. Try inhaling for 4 counts and exhaling for 4 counts
- Anxiety is temporary. It will pass
- Try grounding yourself: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste

stress:
- It's okay to feel stressed. Have you tried taking a break?
- Consider doing something you enjoy to reduce stress
- Exercise can help reduce stress significantly

depression:
- I'm sorry you're feeling this way. Please reach out to someone you trust
- Depression is treatable. Consider talking to a professional
- You are not alone in this. Many people experience depression

sleep:
- Establish a consistent sleep schedule
- Try avoiding screens 1 hour before bed
- Deep breathing exercises can help you fall asleep

self_care:
- Self-care is important. What activities bring you joy?
- Have you been taking care of your physical health?
- Remember to be kind to yourself

relationships:
- Open communication is key in relationships
- It's important to set healthy boundaries
- Consider expressing your feelings honestly and calmly

motivation:
- You are capable of achieving your goals
- Progress over perfection. Small steps count
- Remember why you started. You've got this!

gratitude:
- What are three things you're grateful for today?
- Gratitude can shift your perspective positively
- Appreciating small things can boost your mood

coping:
- What coping strategies work best for you?
- Journaling, meditation, or talking to someone can help
- It's okay to ask for help when you need it

emergency:
- If you're in crisis, please call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- Text HOME to 741741 for Crisis Text Line
- Your life has value. Help is available 24/7

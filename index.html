Daily Wellness Tips

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water throughout the day. Proper hydration supports mental clarity and emotional stability.

🌅 Morning Routine

Start your day with intention. A simple 5-minute routine can set a positive tone.

🚶 Take Breaks

Step away from screens. A short walk can refresh your mind and boost mood.

😴 Sleep Well

Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Sleep is crucial for mental health recovery.
